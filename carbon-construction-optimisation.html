Carbon & Construction Optimisation

External specialist support for clients who need to understand carbon impact, compare technical options and identify practical savings in design, materials, transport and construction processes.

What the service solves

Carbon and ESG requirements are often added late, when the project team has limited time and no dedicated internal specialist. This package supports early screening, option comparison and better tender or design decisions.

Typical scope

Assessment

  • rapid carbon screening,
  • comparison of design or construction variants,
  • review of bill of quantities or BIM data quality.

Optimisation

  • material and process savings,
  • transport and logistics considerations,
  • recommendations for procurement requirements.

Business value

The output is focused on decisions: which option is more defensible, where the most relevant savings are, what should be required in the tender, and which claims need better data before being used externally.
