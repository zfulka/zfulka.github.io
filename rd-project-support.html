Research and innovation project support.

Support for technical R&D projects where sustainability, digitalisation, energy flexibility, BIM/data structures and AI-supported decision tools must be translated into credible work packages, pilots, deliverables and exploitable results.

What I can support

Proposal architecture

Concept structure, work-package logic, deliverables, milestones, KPIs and links between technical objectives, pilots and exploitation.

Technical writing

Drafting and refining technical narratives on BIM, data structures, sustainability, carbon footprint, energy flexibility, digital twins and decision support.

Exploitation and uptake

Translation of project outputs into guidance, tools, training, stakeholder pathways, service concepts and reusable methods.

Selected R&D context

RECONMATIC

R&D work around construction and demolition waste, circular construction workflows, material-flow modelling and digital structures supporting reuse, recycling, recovery and future waste prediction.

FlexRICAN

Energy-chain optimisation for research infrastructures, including renewable-energy assessment, BESS and resilience planning, operational flexibility concepts and decision-support tooling.

RI-SPOND

RI-SPOND is a proposal for integrating sustainability into research infrastructures through digitalisation and operational flexibility. The consortium involves ELI ERIC as coordinator and pilot, CERN, CNRS, EMFL, HFML-FELIX, HZDR, Oxford, University of Manchester, IBM, Energy Pool, ELI ALPS, POLPUR and other partners. The project scope combines RI-specific sustainability methods such as ORLCA, AESA and Scope 3 structures with scientific instrumentation, energy flexibility, Data Lakehouse, federated AI, digital twins and prescriptive maintenance.
