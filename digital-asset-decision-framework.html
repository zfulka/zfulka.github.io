Asset data · prioritisation · decision matrix

Digital Asset & Decision Framework

Support for owners and operators who need to turn fragmented documentation, asset records and technical priorities into a transparent decision framework.

What the service solves

Infrastructure data is often dispersed across PDFs, DWGs, spreadsheets, older passports and undocumented local knowledge. This package helps define a minimum useful data structure and translate it into decision criteria.

Typical scope

Data structure

  • audit of available documentation,
  • minimum asset data structure,
  • classification of objects and attributes.

Decision support

  • prioritisation by condition, risk and cost,
  • carbon and operational relevance where data allows,
  • decision matrix for repairs, investment or digitalisation.

Result

A practical framework that can be used by a municipality, asset owner, project manager or technical department to decide what to inspect, repair, model, procure or budget first.

Send a short description of the asset, documentation, tender or project decision. I will suggest a focused scope and expected output.
