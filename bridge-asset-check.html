Bridge Asset Check

Preliminary technical support for municipalities and infrastructure owners who need a fast, practical view of bridges, footbridges and culverts before ordering diagnostics, design or maintenance work.

What the service solves

Many smaller infrastructure owners do not have a current, structured overview of bridge assets, visible defects or short-term risks. This package provides a practical first step before a full diagnostic campaign or design procurement.

Scope

On-site review

  • visual preliminary review of the object,
  • lightweight-drone photo documentation where useful and safe,
  • record of visible defects and hard-to-access areas.

Output

  • concise technical protocol,
  • photo documentation,
  • recommended next steps for maintenance, diagnostics or design procurement.

Reference framework

The work can be aligned with the logic of bridge evidence and inspection standards used in the Czech environment, including ČSN 73 6220, ČSN 73 6221, ČSN 73 6222, M7 methodology and related technical rules. The preliminary review is not presented as a replacement for legally required formal inspections where those are required.
